# Meowltiverse motion language

Use this skill for animation, particles, camera movement, portal transitions, cat transformations, UI motion, or sound feedback. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/BEHAVIORAL_DESIGN.md`, and the relevant room card first.

For each proposed cue, state the event, player-facing information, emotional tone, duration, and reduced-motion alternative. Favor anticipation, directional impact confirmation, and short recovery beats. Let the cat orb, flippers, collision surfaces, and active physics rule remain distinguishable.

Psychedelic visuals should make a local rule feel wondrous without making the game ambiguous. Encode every rule in at least two channels. Do not use strobing, continuous urgency signals, strong camera shake that hides the ball, or decorative motion that conflicts with a precision shot.

Document new shared visual language or accessibility behavior in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, and validate `prefers-reduced-motion` in the browser when changing presentation.
