# Meowltiverse playtest review

Use this skill when reviewing a play session, survey, issue report, or gameplay recording. Read `docs/PLAYTEST_PROTOCOL.md`, `docs/BEHAVIORAL_DESIGN.md`, and relevant room cards.

Use the session protocol unless the study requires another approved method. Separate observed behavior from interpretation. Evaluate goal comprehension, challenge–skill fit, feedback, failure clarity, sensory comfort, autonomy, and voluntary stopping. Do not infer mental-health conditions or treat time played as the primary success metric.

Report evidence, interpretation, confidence, and one smallest reversible next experiment. Do not recommend broad redesign based on one session. If the team accepts a resulting change, update its room card, tests, and `CHANGELOG.md`.
