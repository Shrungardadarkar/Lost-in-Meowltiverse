# Short playtest protocol

## Goal

Use short sessions to test comprehension, skill–challenge fit, recovery clarity, sensory comfort, and voluntary stopping. Do not measure “time spent” as success.

## Session

1. Explain controls only. Do not explain portals, bells, cats, or the objective unless the player asks.
2. Observe one 5–10 minute play session. Record moments of hesitation, delight, confusion, failure cause, pause use, and voluntary stopping.
3. Ask four questions afterward:
   - What were you trying to do in the last room?
   - When did you feel most in control? Least in control?
   - Did anything feel visually or sonically overwhelming?
   - Did the game give you a satisfying point to stop? Why or why not?
4. Separate observed behavior from player interpretation. Do not diagnose participants or make clinical claims.

## Report format

| Evidence | Interpretation | Confidence | Next experiment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | low / medium / high | |

Recommend one smallest reversible next experiment. Update a room card and `CHANGELOG.md` only after a product change is accepted.
