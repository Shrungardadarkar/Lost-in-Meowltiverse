# Feature review: Midnight Arcade ceiling break

1. **Skill practiced:** Return control, fresh flipper timing, and aiming a physical bank shot at a clear target.
2. **Player choice:** Use either side bank to light the next classic target, or take a safer lower return and try again. The ceiling cannot be opened by score or by passive contact.
3. **Goal and feedback:** Light three warm mechanical targets, then strike the now-cracked ceiling. Each target changes from coral to gold, rings once, and remains visibly lit.
4. **Flow demand:** The opening begins below later-table complexity: ordinary classic geometry and one target at a time establish the flipper-to-target relationship before the full multiverse table appears.
5. **Recovery:** Side guards and lower returns keep the ball in the field; the only loss remains the clearly marked center drain and it restores the same opening feed.
6. **Anti-compulsion boundary:** This is a one-time authored introduction, has no timer, score threshold, randomized reward, or repeated reward loop, and pause remains available.
7. **Reduced motion cue:** Lit targets use stable color and shape; the ceiling changes to a static cracked state and a single calm color wash replaces shake, fragments, and the pull-through.
8. **Satisfied stopping point:** The player can pause from the opening. After the break, the regular table loop retains biome checkpoints and its false-summit stopping point.

## Decision

The first transition is a physical story action, not a cutscene or modal. The player is responsible for breaking the familiar machine and the multiverse appears as the direct consequence of that final shot.
