# Room card: Tide Cathedral table chain

## Intent

- **Biome / world rule:** A single directional current changes the upper orbit; the lower flipper field and gravity remain stable.
- **Player fantasy:** Steer a spirit-cat through a liquid cathedral whose water remembers the direction of every shot.
- **Skill practiced:** Read a current's arrow, choose the matching side orbit, and use a familiar bank when a safer return is needed.
- **Expected session placement:** The second two-table biome loop, after stable Chrome Root banks.
- **Natural stopping relation:** The second lit moon-scoop saves the biome; the portal remains a voluntary detour.

## Flow sequence

| Stage | Layout and behavior | Player feedback | Failure and recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview | A broad translucent current sits above an ordinary lower return lane. | Fixed current arrows and rippling edge indicate the real lateral force. | The non-current bank remains readable. |
| Practice | A wide current sends a shot toward a bumper-and-return arc. | Trail bends with the actual force. | A late or wrong-side shot returns through the lower lane. |
| Mastery shot | The cat's side position rewards choosing the current-facing orbit, then a committed rescue shot. | Rescue marks fall away; moon scoop opens. | The closed scoop gives a gentle return, not a loss. |
| Release | A lit scoop transfers to the sluice table; its portal is optional. | Cyan/coral field folds into the next table. | New table begins from the same predictable feed. |

## Routes and rewards

- Main route: current-facing orbit → cat bank → lit moon scoop.
- Optional route: Liquid Moon portal; three visible stardust grants the disclosed shield and rescue pulse.
- Deterministic reward condition: three committed cat hits light the scoop.
- Story / spirit discovery: a freed cat releases a water-ripple version of the dog's bark.

## Sensory cue sheet

| Event | Visual / animation | Sound | Reduced-motion alternative | Information conveyed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current | Flow ribbon bends toward its actual force. | Soft stereo drift. | Static arrow band. | Lateral push direction. |
| Current-facing bank | Thin trail arcs into the target lane. | Glassy rail note. | Chevron and route highlight. | Correct orbit choice. |
| Scoop open | Moon ring fills with cyan light. | Three-note water chime. | High-contrast open center. | Transfer is available. |

## Behavioral review and validation

Autonomy: portal and expressive orbit are optional. Competence: one new force is visible before it matters. Relatedness: the bark fragment is gentle and non-urgent. Sensory load: current force is encoded by arrows, shape, motion, and sound. Compulsion risk: no time pressure or random water route. Playtest question: can players predict which way the current will bend the ball? Tests validate one current table has a safe non-current route, a current route, one cat, and one transfer.
