# Room card: Tide Cathedral route variants

## Intent

- **Biome / rule:** Stable climb gravity with visibly lateral currents. The Liquid Moon portal alone lowers gravity.
- **Fantasy and skill:** Read a living current and aim a bank that crosses it.
- **Placement and rest:** Orientation, remix, and optional portal beats before a rescue and biome rest.

## Flow sequence

| Stage | Layout and behavior | Feedback | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview | Tide Channel or Crosscurrent shows a wide stream and a quiet rail. | Current ribbons point along the actual force. | Lower edge returns toward a flipper. |
| Practice | A bumper gives one predictable lateral deflection. | Rebound arrow remains readable. | Wrong angle loses height without a surprise drain. |
| Mastery | Tide Choir or Eddy asks for a crosscurrent bank; Moon Door or Sluice offers an optional portal approach. | Matching chevrons and a distinct portal rim. | The main upward route remains open. |
| Release | A safe upper rail feeds the next segment. | Motion settles as the target leaves view. | The next room receives a central or side feed. |

## Routes and rewards

- Main route: bank through the current toward the next rescue.
- Express route: matching lateral entry into the upper rail for a faster climb and collectible.
- Discovery route: hit the Tide portal directly to enter Liquid Moon.
- Reward: visible collectibles and portal objectives only; no random bell award.
- Story: a familiar bark travels through the current.

## Sensory cue sheet

| Event | Visual | Sound | Reduced motion | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current | Ribbons lean in force direction | Soft wash | Static arrow ribbons | Lateral push |
| Correct bank | Brief direction streak | Short chime | Rail outline and rebound arrow | Route confirmed |
| Portal | Aqua ring with current marks | Low swell | Stable ring and marks | Low gravity ahead |

## Behavioral review and validation

Autonomy: portal is optional. Competence: one force is introduced before combining it with a bank. Relatedness: the dog trail stays hopeful. Sensory load: the ball and flippers keep highest contrast. Compulsion risk: no timer or rare reward. Playtest question: can players predict the current direction before a shot? Tests must keep two rescue modules, one portal, valid clearance, and reachable safe returns in each Tide chapter.
