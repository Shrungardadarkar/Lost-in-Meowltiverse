# Room card: Clockwork route variants

## Intent

- **Biome / rule:** Stable climb gravity; visible rhythm gates cycle on simulation time. The Hours Between portal alone slows time.
- **Fantasy and skill:** Watch a clock face, then commit a shot through an opening.
- **Placement and rest:** Orientation, timed remix, optional portal choice, rescue, then a calm biome pause.

## Flow sequence

| Stage | Layout and behavior | Feedback | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview | Mandala Bloom or Pendulum shows a redirect and one wide gate. | Open shape and color match the real collision state. | Closed gate bounces toward a flipper. |
| Practice | The first mandala sends the orb into a marked bank. | Petal direction and rebound arrow. | Direct main route remains valid. |
| Mastery | Clock Gate or Lattice varies the rhythm; Time Door or Hourglass offers an optional portal shot. | Gate aperture and chevrons stay visible. | Missed portal returns to the main climb. |
| Release | Upper rail hands off to a readable next feed. | Short warm accent, then stillness. | No forced speed increase. |

## Routes and rewards

- Main route: time a gate or choose the safe side bank.
- Express route: align a mandala redirect with the upper rail.
- Discovery route: strike the Time portal for a slow-time room.
- Reward: visible collectibles and a disclosed portal objective.
- Story: the dog's lost toy appears between seconds.

## Sensory cue sheet

| Event | Visual | Sound | Reduced motion | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gate | Aperture and color use simulation time | Quiet open/closed motif | Same still aperture and color | Collision state now |
| Mandala | Petal spin and arrow | Gentle chime | Static directional sectors | Redirect direction |
| Portal | Violet ring with clock ticks | Slow bell | Stable ticks and ring | Slow time ahead |

## Behavioral review and validation

Autonomy: optional portal and safe bank. Competence: predictable cycles with a recoverable closed gate. Relatedness: dog clue is discovered, not rushed. Sensory load: no clock flash or strobe. Compulsion risk: no countdown reward. Playtest question: can players predict whether a shot will pass the gate? Tests must cover visual/physics clock agreement and keep two rescue modules plus an optional portal in every chapter.
