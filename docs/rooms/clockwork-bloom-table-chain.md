# Room card: Clockwork Bloom table chain

## Intent

- **Biome / world rule:** One gate rhythm or one mandala redirect changes the upper route; flipper timing remains stable.
- **Player fantasy:** Send a spirit-cat through a flowering clock that opens one petal-wide path at a time.
- **Skill practiced:** Observe a calm cycle, cradle or return safely, then commit to an open gate or marked redirect.
- **Expected session placement:** Third two-table biome loop and pre-summit integration.
- **Natural stopping relation:** The second hourglass-scoop saves the biome and leads to the false summit without a forced continuation.

## Flow sequence

| Stage | Layout and behavior | Player feedback | Failure and recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview | A wide gate and a mandala show their open state and redirect sectors before the cat route. | Aperture, gold/rose state, and sector arrow agree with physics. | The lower bank bypasses the timing line. |
| Practice | One mandala feeds a broad rail. | Redirect arrow and brief chime reveal its exit vector. | A miss falls through a standard return lane. |
| Mastery shot | A cat behind the gate/redirect is struck with one committed shot at the readable moment. | Rescue marks and scoop state change persistently. | Closed gate gives a soft bounce; no hidden timing loss. |
| Release | The opened hourglass scoop transfers to the summit-facing table. | Brief petal fold, then a stable new field. | Predictable upper feed restores flipper control. |

## Routes and rewards

- Main route: safe bank → read gate/mandala → committed cat hit → lit scoop.
- Optional route: The Hours Between portal; slow time is previewed by clock ticks and returns to a known feed.
- Deterministic reward condition: three committed cat hits light the scoop.
- Story / spirit discovery: the dog’s lost ball appears as a quiet time echo.

## Behavioral review and validation

Autonomy: timing gate has a safe bypass and portal is optional. Competence: cycle is readable rather than reaction-speed driven. Relatedness: rescue resolves into a quiet dog-memory clue. Sensory load: no strobe or clock urgency; every rule has a static cue. Compulsion risk: no countdown, random timing, or repeat gate. Playtest question: can a player describe when and why their shot passed the gate? Tests validate a gate/mandala table has a bypass, a cat, and an upper transfer.
