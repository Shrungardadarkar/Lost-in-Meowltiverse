# Room card: Spirit rescue threshold

## Intent

- **Biome / rule:** Shared main-climb encounter; stable physics.
- **Fantasy:** A possessed cat's distortion holds the next part of the world closed until the spirit is freed.
- **Skill:** Return a shot to the marked cat three times, using the flippers and approach banks.
- **Placement and rest:** Two encounters per adventure; the second leads to biome rest.

## Flow sequence

| Stage | Layout and behavior | Feedback | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview | A thin aurora boundary appears above the active cat. | Boundary and cat share color and three rescue marks. | The cat remains in camera view. |
| Practice | First impact removes one mark; boundary safely redirects any premature climb. | One mark fades, boundary softens. | A rebound returns toward a flipper; no bell is lost at the boundary. |
| Mastery | Three deliberate impacts free the cat. | Distortion peels away; boundary clears. | Misses can be retried without a timer. |
| Release | The upper route opens. | Brief calm accent and a dog-trail clue. | The next room receives the orb through ordinary physics. |

## Routes and rewards

- Main route: free the active cat, then climb through its cleared threshold.
- Optional route: bank for collectibles while the cat remains visible.
- Portal: separate optional module between the rescues; never replaces a rescue.
- Deterministic reward: three impacts free one cat; two cats complete the adventure.
- Story: each cat returns as a friendly spirit and reveals a small clue.

## Sensory cue sheet

| Event | Visual | Sound | Reduced motion | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boundary touched | Brief ripple down toward cat | Low soft chime | Stable line brightens once | Rescue still needed |
| Cat impact | One mark fades | Short warm note | Mark count changes | Progress 1/3, 2/3 |
| Liberation | Boundary dissolves | Resolving chord | Line vanishes without animation | Upper route open |

## Behavioral review and validation

Autonomy: the player can pause, retry, or take a side bank. Competence: the requirement is visible and deterministic. Relatedness: liberation is a gentle transformation. Sensory load: thin boundary avoids hiding the shot. Compulsion risk: no countdown or variable reward. Playtest question: can a new player explain why the boundary returned the orb? Tests must confirm an unfinished cat remains visible and that freeing it opens the route.
