# Room card: The Hours Between

## Intent

- **Biome / world rule:** Clockwork Bloom portal; only this pocket room slows simulation time.
- **Fantasy and skill:** Plan a shot through three calmly cycling clock gates while time stretches.
- **Placement and rest:** Optional discovery route during a Clockwork adventure; returns to a safe main-climb feed.

## Flow sequence

| Stage | Layout and behavior | Feedback | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview | Violet portal ring has permanent clock ticks. | Silhouette, accent color, and soft bell imply time change. | Main route remains open if missed. |
| Practice | One broad gate opens and closes above the first flipper feed. | Aperture and color use the exact collision clock. | Closed gate returns a gentle bounce. |
| Mastery | Three separated gates, alternating mandalas, and three visible stardust ask for planned trajectories. | Stable sector arrows and collected targets. | No timer-based reward or forced route. |
| Release | Upper return ring sits opposite Liquid Moon's exit. | Room palette settles at return. | Automatic safety return after 30 seconds. |

## Routes and rewards

- Main route: skip the portal and continue the normal climb.
- Discovery route: hit the marked portal, gather three stardust, and leave through the upper ring.
- Deterministic reward: three stardust restore one bell when below seven and grant one shield plus one rescue pulse.
- Story: a dog toy appears caught between moments.

## Sensory cue sheet

| Event | Visual | Sound | Reduced motion | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portal | Clock ticks around the rim | Low bell | Static rim and ticks | Slow time ahead |
| Gate | Live aperture and color | Quiet wait tone on blocked shot | Same live shape/color without decorative rotation | Pass or bounce now |
| Mandala | Direction sector and petal drift | Gentle chime | Static direction arrow | Expected redirect |

## Behavioral review and validation

Autonomy: entrance is a physical optional shot. Competence: one slow-time rule with three observable gates. Relatedness: the dog clue is gentle and hopeful. Sensory load: gates and orb remain prominent. Compulsion risk: no scarce or random reward. Playtest question: can players predict a gate's state before contact? Tests cover distinct layout, temporary physics, and return feed.
