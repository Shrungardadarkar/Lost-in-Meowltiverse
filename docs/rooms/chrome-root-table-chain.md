# Room card: Chrome Root table chain

## Intent

- **Biome / world rule:** Chrome Root; stable base physics and luminous root rails reward correctly aimed banks.
- **Player fantasy:** Guide a spirit-cat through a living impossible pinball tree, growing one new route at a time.
- **Skill practiced:** Return control, a banked rescue shot, and an earned upper-scoop transfer.
- **Expected session placement:** Opening two-table biome loop, then recursively remixed in later universes.
- **Natural stopping relation:** The second successful scoop saves the biome and leads to a false-summit-capable next world; pause is always safe at the lower return feed.

## Flow sequence

| Stage | Layout and behavior | Player feedback | Failure and recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview | Two rails frame a possessed cat above the flippers; an upper root scoop is dim. | Rescue marks and the dim scoop establish cause before the shot. | A missed bank descends into an inward lane. |
| Practice | Low side banks give safe angled returns. | Chevron direction and rebound arrow show the achieved vector. | The lower guards feed one near miss back to a flipper. |
| Mastery shot | Three cat impacts free the spirit and light the upper scoop. | The spirit clears, root ring brightens, and the scoop gains a stable open core. | An early scoop contact gives a soft downward return rather than a drain. |
| Release | A deliberate upper shot enters the scoop and feeds the next table from above. | Brief color warp and root growth communicate a new chamber, without a modal. | The next table begins at a predictable flipper feed. |

## Routes and rewards

- Main route: bank into the cat target, then shoot the lit upper scoop.
- Optional route: a portal scoop branches into a temporary pocket table and returns through a known feed.
- Portal behavior, if any: temporary, telegraphed Tide or Time rule; no main-route requirement.
- Deterministic reward condition: free the current cat to open the upper scoop; free two cats and take the second transfer to save the biome.
- Story / spirit discovery: each freed cat leaves a clear fragment of the dog's route.

## Sensory cue sheet

| Event | Visual / animation | Sound | Reduced-motion alternative | Information conveyed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rescue mark | One collar-like mark breaks from the cat. | Short warm chime. | Static remaining marks. | One of three impacts landed. |
| Scoop locked | Root ring remains dim and closed. | Soft wood knock on contact. | Dark closed center and lock shape. | Continue the rescue first. |
| Scoop opened | Roots unfurl into a bright circular lane. | Rising three-note tone. | Bright open core and fixed arrow. | A transfer shot is now available. |
| Transfer | Cat stretches into a root-lit upper feed. | Brief whoosh. | Color and lane swap only. | The next table has begun. |

## Behavioral review

- Autonomy: portal and expressive bank routes are optional; the clear main route remains available.
- Competence: one objective, one lit transfer condition, and known lower return paths make failure readable.
- Relatedness: rescue frees rather than defeats a cat and visibly advances the dog's trail.
- Sensory load: scoop state is communicated by geometry, color, and sound; it never hides the flippers.
- Compulsion-risk check: no timed transfer, streak, random drop, or score gate.
- Playtest question: can a new player explain why the scoop is locked and deliberately return to a flipper after missing it?

## Implementation and validation

- Module / object changes: fixed-height table anchors, dim/open transfer scoops, table-local cat gating, lower return lanes, and transfer-fed table changes.
- Tests to add or revise: locked scoop rejects entry, a freed cat opens its scoop, transfer sets the next stable flipper feed, and two transfers complete one biome.
- Documentation to update: changelog, decisions, project context, and this room card.
