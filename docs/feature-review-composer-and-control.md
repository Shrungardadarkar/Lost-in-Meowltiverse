# Feature review: controlled feeds and safe room composition

1. **Skill:** Read a return feed, choose a timed shot, a brief cradle, or a cross-flipper pass.
2. **Choice:** Left/right timing determines a route; optional portal and express shots remain voluntary. Room variation changes a shot's geometry, never its required rescue count.
3. **Goal and feedback:** Two visible cats per adventure are always reachable objectives. Shot arrows, rail chevrons, gate state, and rescue marks show immediate results.
4. **Challenge match:** Each five-room chapter retains orientation, rescue, remix, portal choice, and rescue. Bounded variation selects only within the same role and biome at a future module boundary.
5. **Recovery:** A short held cradle expires into a gentle feed; misses reach a lower flipper or the marked centre drain and saved elevation.
6. **Compulsion boundary:** No rewards depend on session duration or streaks. Difficulty variation can be switched off and reset from pause; progress is saved at biome completion.
7. **Reduced motion:** Gate color and shape use simulation time; static chevrons and current direction remain. Camera punches and decorative rotation are removed.
8. **Rest:** Ordinary biome completion carries the orb quietly into the next playable biome; it never asks for a dismiss tap. The false summit stops the simulation at a natural ending and requires a fresh flipper tap to continue, while pause remains available at all times.

The composer rejects a module if its objective role changes, its geometry is invalid, or its entry and exit contracts cannot connect to its neighbors. A safe authored variant is used instead. The system records seeds and replays only local input traces for development; it collects no remote behavior.
